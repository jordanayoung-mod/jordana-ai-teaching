After booking

What happens after you book

Once you've paid and chosen a time, you'll have a video link. Do the prep for your level before we meet, so we can start on your task.

  1. You pay on the provider's page and keep the confirmation. A formal Israeli receipt or tax invoice is emailed once the invoicing tool is connected.
  2. You choose a time. Bookings open the week of Sunday 29 November 2026, Sunday to Thursday, 11:00 to 20:00 Israel time, with lunch 12:00 to 12:30 and Mondays also blocked 16:30 to 18:30.
  3. The booking is added to my Google Calendar. A busy event on that calendar blocks the same time here.
  4. Open the prep page for your level and fill in the goals form. I read it before the session.
  5. We meet. We check the starting point, agree one or two goals, you build while I guide, you save an artifact, and we write three next steps.
  6. After the session I send a recap with the artifact, a short feedback note, and an invitation to leave a review.

Some of those emails may be sent by hand until the automations are connected. The prep pages are ready now.
