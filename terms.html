The service

I sell live, one-to-one, online teaching sessions in English, small-group cohorts, and, separately, custom workflow builds for companies. A one-to-one place, a cohort seat, and a company quote are different offers. Company work isn't sold from the 40-session inventory.

Price

Prices are in Israeli shekels (ILS). The figure on the payment page is the figure you pay.

Private coaching prices step up as places sell: 8 sessions at ₪400, then 8 at ₪600, then 8 at ₪750, then 8 at ₪950, then 8 at ₪1,200. You pay the step that's open when the payment succeeds. Your level doesn't change that price. A cohort seat is ₪600 per person, with a maximum of 15 people, and it doesn't use those 40 places.

How a booking is confirmed

You pay first. A time is confirmed when the booking tool accepts it after payment. Unpaid holds are not a confirmed session. The seller is Jordana Young. A business registration number and address will be added here once the business is registered.

Cancellation and refunds

The written cancellation rules are not final yet. The working plan is a free reschedule up to 24 hours before the session, from the link in your confirmation. Ask for the rule in writing before you pay if you need it. This section will be updated before bookings open, in line with the Consumer Protection Law rules for distance transactions.

Your part

Bring a task you are allowed to share. Do not paste secrets or other people's personal data into AI tools during the session. You keep the artifact we build in your own account.

Company builds

A quote is an offer to talk, not a contract. The contract is the written proposal you accept. Scope, price, and timing live there.
