Privacy

Privacy Policy

Draft, dated 20 November 2026. This is not legal advice. It will be checked against the Israeli Privacy Protection Law, 1981, including Amendment 13, and replaced if counsel changes it. Bookings are due to open the week of 29 November 2026.

This site is run by Jordana Young as a personal business. It is not a Microsoft site. The contact form is the way to make a privacy request until a public email address is published. Email me

What I collect

  • Identity and contact details you type into a form: name, email, phone, company, and role.
  • Your questionnaire answers, score, level, and learning path.
  • Booking details you give the booking tool, and the fact that a payment succeeded. Card numbers stay on the payment provider's page.
  • Goals, reviews, and messages you send.
  • The consent boxes you ticked, the wording next to them, and the time you sent the form.
  • If you accept analytics, the analytics tool's usage data. If you reject it, that script doesn't load.

Why

To answer you, recommend a level, take payment, book a time, prepare the session, and publish a review you agreed to. Marketing email or WhatsApp is sent only if you tick the optional box. That box is separate from the box that lets me contact you about the session or the quote. You can opt out of marketing by writing to me, and I will stop.

Who else processes it

These are the tools named in the site configuration. When a tool is still "not connected", it is not receiving data yet.

  • Form host (not connected yet). Will store form submissions.
  • Israeli payment provider (not connected yet). Will take payment. This website never receives the card number.
  • Booking tool (not connected yet). Will take the time you choose and send the video link.
  • Google Calendar. Holds the booked session on Jordana's calendar.

LetsGo receives a review only if you tick the separate LetsGo box. Until LetsGo offers an automatic import, that handoff is an email or a form, not a live connection from this website.

How long it is kept

Working plan, to be confirmed: enquiries from people who don't book are deleted or anonymised after 24 months. Booking and payment records are kept for the period Israeli tax law requires, often 7 years. Reviews stay until you ask for them to be removed. The consent record stays with the submission.

Your requests

You can ask to see, correct, or delete your details, subject to records I must keep for tax or for a booking that already happened. Write via the contact page. If you are in the EU or UK, say so in the message and I will handle the request with the GDPR rights that apply, including a reply within a month where the law requires it.

Cookies and storage

The questionnaire keeps your answers in this browser's session storage until you close the tab, so the results page can show them. The analytics choice, if you make one, is stored in local storage on your device. Analytics cookies or scripts run only after you press Accept, and only if analytics is switched on in the configuration. It is off by default.
