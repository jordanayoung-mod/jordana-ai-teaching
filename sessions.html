The five price steps

    Prices are in Israeli shekels (ILS). The figure on the payment page is the figure you pay.

    What you get, at every level

    • A live, one-to-one session online, in English.
    • A level and a learning path from the questionnaire, so we start from where you are.
    • You drive. I guide. You leave with an artifact and three next steps.
    • A prep page for your level, and a short goals form I read before we meet.
    • A calendar invite on my Google Calendar, with your name and level in the title once the booking tool is connected.
    • Payment in ILS by credit card or Bit, on the payment provider's page. Then you choose the time.

    The booking page shows how long the session is. There is a short gap after each one so sessions don't run into each other.
