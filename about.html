About

I help shape Microsoft's AI agent builder. Now I'll help you put AI to work.I study the moments where people stall with AI. Then I help you get past them.

JY

Since 2024 I've been a Senior User Researcher on Microsoft Copilot Studio, the platform companies use to build their own AI agents. It serves 450,000 organisations and more than 30 million custom agents.

My job is to sit with the people building those agents, see exactly where they get stuck, and work with Copilot Studio's product, design and engineering teams to fix it. I've published 21 studies on AI agents at Microsoft, tested with builders in four countries, and received Microsoft's 2026 Innovation Catalyst Award.

So I know the exact moments where people stall with AI, and I know how to get them past those moments quickly. Before Microsoft, I spent 20 years studying how people really use products, at Meta, SodaStream (PepsiCo) and Ipsos.

How I teach

I meet you where you are. We pick one or two goals tied to a real task, and you do the hands-on work while I guide you. You leave with an artifact, such as a prompt you can reuse, a custom assistant, or a workflow, plus a short plan for what to do next.

Sessions are live and one to one, online. The booking page shows the length and the video link.
