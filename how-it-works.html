How it works

Pay first. Then choose a time.

The questionnaire tells us your level. The price is whatever step is open when you pay. After payment, you pick a slot and the time goes onto my Google Calendar.

The steps

  1. You leave your name, email, and phone, and you choose whether I may send marketing messages. Service messages about your booking are separate.
  2. You answer 12 questions. Ten are scored. One asks what you want help with. One chooses a path.
  3. You see a level: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert, plus a path such as writing or research.
  4. You pay the current price on a secure page. I never see your card number. Bit and Israeli credit cards are the intended methods.
  5. You open the booking page and choose a time. Put your name and level in the form if they are not already filled in, so the calendar title can read like this: AI Session: Dana Cohen (Beginner).
  6. You get a video link, a prep page for your level, and a goals form. I read the form before we meet.

When you can book

Bookings open the week of Sunday 29 November 2026. No times are offered before that date. The booking page shows times in your time zone, and the confirmation also states the Israel time.

Friday and Saturday are closed. Lunch is blocked every open day, so a session cannot overlap 12:00 to 12:30. Monday afternoons are also blocked from 16:30 to 18:30.

Google Calendar

When a booking is confirmed, it is added to my Google Calendar. Busy times on that calendar are blocked on the booking page, including sessions booked through other tools, so we do not double-book. A reschedule moves the event. A cancellation clears it. I connect the calendar inside the booking tool (Cal.com or Calendly).
