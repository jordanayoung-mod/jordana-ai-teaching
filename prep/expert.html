Expert prep

Before your expert session

Same setup as an advanced session, plus access to the systems you want to connect and a non-sensitive sample of the data.

Setup checklist

  • Have the relevant tools ready: chat tools, custom assistants, an automation tool such as Zapier or Make, or an agent builder.
  • Prepare one concrete workflow to build or improve.
  • Bring logins or permissions for the systems you want to connect, and check you can sign in before we meet.
  • Have non-sensitive sample data ready. Do not bring live customer data or secrets.

A short read

A useful agent pattern has a trigger, written instructions, one or two tools it may call, and a check before anything is sent or saved. We will build the smallest version of that pattern that still does the job.

Safety

Do not paste passwords, identity numbers, health details, or confidential work files into an AI tool. If you want to use something from work, check your employer's AI rules first, and bring a non-confidential sample if you are unsure.
