Advanced prep

Before your advanced session

Come with the tools installed and one workflow you want to build or improve. We will spend the session on that workflow.

Setup checklist

  • Have the relevant tools ready. Examples: ChatGPT or Claude, custom GPTs or Projects, an automation tool such as Zapier or Make, or an agent builder.
  • Prepare one concrete workflow or use case to build or improve in the session.
  • Share sample inputs ahead if you can, through the goals form. Keep them non-confidential.

A short read

A custom assistant is a saved set of instructions, plus any files or tools you attach, so you don't re-explain the job every time. We will decide what the assistant must do, what it must refuse, and how you will check its output.

Safety

Do not paste passwords, identity numbers, health details, or confidential work files into an AI tool. If you want to use something from work, check your employer's AI rules first, and bring a non-confidential sample if you are unsure.
