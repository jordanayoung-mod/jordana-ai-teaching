FAQ

Questions before you book

Does my level change the price?

No. Everyone in this release pays the price that is open when they pay: 400 ILS for the first 10 sessions, then 600, then 800, then 1,200. The questionnaire changes your prep and what we work on. See the current price.

Which level am I?

Answer the 12 questions. The score maps to Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert. If you have never used an AI tool, the result stays at Beginner. Find your level.

What tools do I need?

A laptop, a video call that can share a screen, and an AI account. Beginners can use a free ChatGPT or Claude account. Other levels bring the tools they already use. Each prep page lists the exact setup.

What language is the session?

English. The site, the forms, and the session are in English.

Is it online or in person?

Online, one to one. The video link is in the calendar invite. Zoom, Google Meet, or Teams is set in the booking tool.

How do I pay?

You pay first, in Israeli shekels, on a page run by an Israeli payment provider. Credit cards and Bit are the intended methods. Then you choose a time. This website does not see your card number. Book and pay.

When can I book?

The week of Sunday 29 November 2026, then ongoing. Sunday to Thursday, 11:00 to 20:00 Israel time. Lunch is 12:00 to 12:30. Mondays are also blocked 16:30 to 18:30. Friday and Saturday are closed. See the hours.

What if I need to cancel?

The written cancellation rules are not final yet. The working plan is a free reschedule up to 24 hours before the session. Ask me for the rule in writing before you pay if you need it. The final text will be on the Terms page before bookings open.

Will I get a receipt?

The payment provider emails a confirmation. A formal Israeli receipt or tax invoice is sent once the invoicing tool is connected to that provider. Keep the confirmation either way.

How is my data used?

To reply to you, prepare your session, take payment, and book the time. Marketing messages are sent only if you tick the optional box. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Is the site accessible?

It is built to Israeli Standard 5568, based on WCAG 2.0 Level AA. The accessibility statement tells you how to ask for help.

Do you build workflows for companies?

Yes. That work is quoted separately and does not use these session prices. Get a quote for a custom build.

What if the 40 sessions sell out?

The price card switches to a waitlist for the next release. Join the waitlist.
