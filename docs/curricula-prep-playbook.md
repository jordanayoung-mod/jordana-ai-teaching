# Jordana Young AI Platform: Cohort Curricula, Prep Packages & Playbook

## Part 1: Cohort Course Facilitator Guides

### Course 1: AI Starter (Beginner)
- **Schedule:** Sunday nights, 3 weekly sessions (Sessions 1 & 2: 90 minutes; Session 3: 60-minute live clinic and wrap-up). 2 cohorts in December 2026.
- **Format:** Max 15 students, live online via Zoom with 3-person breakout rooms and a private group chat between sessions.
- **Core Promise:** Go from "where do I even start?" to using AI with confidence for real tasks every week.

#### Outcomes
1. Give AI clear instructions that produce useful results on the first try.
2. Draft emails, summaries, and everyday plans in half the time.
3. Know what personal or work information is safe to share and what to keep private.
4. Spot when AI is guessing before relying on its answer.
5. Leave with three saved, reusable prompts used every week.

#### Session Run-of-Show
- **Session 1 (90 mins): Speaking AI's Language Without Overthinking.** Live Demo (Role + Context + Format), Breakout Lab (3-person groups rewriting a real task), Privacy & Safety ("Postcard Rule"), Artifact: The 3-Part Ask Cheat Sheet.
- **Session 2 (90 mins): Writing in Your Voice & Spotting Mistakes.** Group Chat Debrief, Live Demo (Feeding 2 writing samples + spotting hallucinations), Breakout Lab (Tone matching), Artifact: Personal Voice Prompt.
- **Session 3 (60 mins): Live Clinic, Capstone & Next Steps.** Capstone Check-In, Hot-Seat Live Clinic on student-submitted tasks, Bridge to AI at Work (Intermediate). Artifact: Personal Starter Kit (3 tested prompts).

### Course 2: AI at Work (Intermediate)
- **Schedule:** Wednesday nights, 4 weekly 90-minute sessions. 1 cohort in December 2026.
- **Format:** Max 15 students, live online via Zoom with breakout rooms and private group chat.
- **Core Promise:** Turn hit-and-miss prompting into a personal prompt library and one reliable weekly workflow.

#### Outcomes
1. Build a structured personal prompt library with bracketed inputs.
2. Upload PDFs, spreadsheets, and meeting transcripts to extract accurate insights fast.
3. Use 3-step prompt chaining for complex work projects.
4. Build and test one repeatable weekly workflow saving at least 2 hours per week.

#### Session Run-of-Show
- **Session 1 (90 mins):** Building Your Personal Prompt Library. Artifact: Starter Prompt Library Template (5 prompts).
- **Session 2 (90 mins):** Working Safely With PDFs, Spreadsheets & Notes. Artifact: Document & Meeting Extraction Prompt Pack.
- **Session 3 (90 mins):** Chaining Steps for Complex Work (Outline -> Critique -> Final Draft). Artifact: 3-Step Workflow Blueprint.
- **Session 4 (90 mins):** Capstone Workflow Build & Demo Day. Artifact: One Tested Weekly Work Workflow saving 2+ hours/week.

## Part 2: Automated Post-Booking Prep Packages & 1-Page Brief

### Beginner Prep Email
Subject: You're booked! Here is your 5-minute prep for our AI session
1. Create a free AI account on chatgpt.com or claude.ai and log in on your laptop.
2. Answer 3 quick questions in the intake form so your session is tailored to your goals.

### Intermediate Prep Email
Subject: You're booked! Quick prep for our AI session
1. Gather 2 or 3 real recurring tasks (with sensitive names/numbers removed).
2. Complete the 2-minute session goals form.

### Advanced & Expert Prep Email
Subject: You're booked! Prep checklist for our build session
1. Have your paid AI workspace (ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro, Copilot Studio, or n8n/Make) logged in.
2. Stage 3 to 5 clean, non-confidential sample files in one folder.
3. Fill out the 2-minute workflow brief.
