# Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026. This is a draft for review by an Israeli lawyer before bookings open.

## Who I am

This website is run by Jordana Young as an independent personal business in Israel. It isn't a Microsoft website. I'm responsible for the personal information collected here under the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, and its regulations, including Amendment 13.

## What I collect

Details you type into a form: your first name and email, and for company quotes your name, work email, company, team size and workflow description.

Your AI level check answers, your score and level, and the area you'd like to start with.

Booking and payment status. Card details stay on the payment provider's secure page and never reach this website.

Whether you ticked the optional marketing box, with the date and the wording you agreed to.

## Why I use it

To show your result and send your roadmap, to manage the waitlist and bookings, to prepare your sessions, to reply to quote requests and to meet my tax and accounting duties. I send marketing emails only if you tick the optional box.

## Who processes it for me

Framer (hosts this website), n8n (receives form submissions and runs my automations), Google Workspace (Sheets, Calendar and Gmail, to keep records, schedule sessions and send emails), an Israeli payment provider (to be confirmed) and Zoom (for live sessions). Some of these services store information outside Israel, with the safeguards the law requires.

## How long I keep it

Enquiries from people who don't book are deleted or anonymised after 24 months. Booking and payment records are kept for as long as Israeli tax law requires, usually 7 years.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time, apart from records I must keep by law. Send me a message and I'll reply within 30 days.

## Marketing emails (Amendment 40)

Under section 30A of the Israeli Communications Law (Telecommunications and Broadcasting), 5742-1982, known as Amendment 40, I only send marketing emails if you've clearly agreed by ticking the optional box. The box is never ticked for you. Every marketing email is marked as an advertisement, shows my name and contact details and has a simple unsubscribe link. You can also reply asking to be removed at any time, and I'll stop.

## Cookies

This site uses only what Framer needs to run it. I don't use advertising cookies. If I add analytics that need cookies, I'll ask for your consent first.

## Questions?

If anything here is unclear, or you'd like to make a request about your information, message me and I'll help.
