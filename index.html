    How a session happens

    Five steps. You do the first two on this site, then you pay, then you choose a time.

    1

    Tell me how to reach you

    Name, email, and phone, plus a separate choice about marketing messages.

    2

    Answer 12 questions

    They take a few minutes. The score recommends a level and a learning path.

    3

    See your level

    Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert. The price on the page stays the current price.

    4

    Pay, then book

    Pay in ILS by card or Bit. Then choose a time. Bookings open the week of Sunday 29 November 2026.

    5

    Prepare, then meet

    Do the short prep for your level. We meet online and you leave with an artifact and a next step.

    What we can work on

    The last question asks which area you want. We use that path in the session, on your own task.

    Work productivity

    Email, meeting notes, and updates you have to write again next week.

    Writing

    Drafts, rewrites, and a voice you want the tool to keep.

    Research

    Sharper questions, and a way to check the answer.

    Images

    Pictures you can use, with prompts you can change.

    Agents and automation

    One repeated process, built as an assistant or an automation.

    AI at home

    Planning, learning, or organising information you actually use.
